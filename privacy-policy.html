Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Excuse Me! - Excuse Generator ("the App") is developed by FunApps. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

The App works entirely on your device. All excuses are generated and displayed locally on your phone and are never transmitted to any server.

Data Storage

The App does not store any personal data. Excuse content is bundled within the App itself and no user-generated data is saved to disk, databases, cloud storage, or any form of remote data persistence.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Sharing Feature

The App includes "Share" and "Copy" features that use your device's native sharing and clipboard functionality. When you share or copy an excuse, the content is handled directly by your device's operating system. We do not intercept, store, or have access to shared or copied content.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App is suitable for all ages and collects no data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
